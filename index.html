02 — About

About

Neil Morash is a live-looping musician based in Halifax, Nova Scotia. On a Boss RC-505mkII loop station he builds the music as he goes: guitar, beatboxing, and vocals, stacked live.

He lives and works out of a converted Sprinter van that doubles as a mobile studio, and he films Sprinter Sessions there. He also practices poi and flow arts. He co-organizes Ramble On Festival in Nova Scotia and will host and perform there in August 2027.
